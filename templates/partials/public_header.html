Measured before writing this: page logo lang toggle trial CTA -> /partners/ landing/index.html full lockup yes yes no landing/pricing.html mark + wordmark no yes no landing/partners.html bare text no no (self) landing/partner_*.html bare text no no yes Two consequences a visitor could see: /partners/ and /partners/apply/ offered no way to change language and no trial CTA, and **nothing anywhere linked to /partners/** — the page shipped in 2A and was reachable only by typing its URL, because its tests call the URL directly and so nothing ever failed. `landing/*` templates are standalone documents by convention (§L.1.2) — no `{% extends %}`. So this include carries its own scoped styles rather than assuming a base stylesheet, and every colour falls back to the declared identity in tokens.css (navy #003366, green #10B981) when a host page does not define the variable. That also settles the §L.1.1 drift where the public pages rendered a different navy (#073763) from the dashboard. Guarded by tests/test_public_shell.py, which renders every public URL and asserts the shell elements are present on all of them. {% endcomment %}
